Exercise guidance and media sources

Kinra combines independently authored English, Bosnian and German movement guidance with the preserved catalogue metadata. No FitConnect exercise instructions, photographs, video or expert-review claims were copied. Catalogue source attribution records where names and categories were observed; it is separate from guide authorship.

The written guides describe movement families with equipment, grip, posture and variant notes. They are general education, not individually assessed technique or treatment prescriptions. Named clinical protocols, postoperative programs and traction require a personal plan. Automated movement-family options are not clinical equivalents. No clinician or trainer review is claimed.

The 21 October 2026 documentation review adds reviewed primary, secondary and stabilizing muscle groups, 30 distinct variations and eight editable gentle-movement examples. Muscle filters match primary and secondary groups; stabilizers are shown separately. Supplied exercise topics informed independently rewritten guidance in all three languages; supplied prose and private correspondence are not published. The original 778 catalogue identities and their capture provenance remain preserved. No universal postoperative schedule, soreness-prevention claim or clinical-review claim is imported. Movement, breath and roller-pass counts are not seconds or automatically recorded durations.

Photographs are from yuhonas/free-exercise-db at commit f00c92c7dcf1216a928a52c3706c7ce8e2f71ed5. The publisher describes the dataset and imagery as public domain and permits using them locally. They are supplied under the Unlicense. Original image paths and SHA-256 checksums are retained in the repository audit manifest. Identity matches are exact names or a deliberately authored crosswalk; there is no fuzzy image matching. Third-party photos are examples and may show a different person, model of equipment or range of motion.

Original Kinra SVGs are simplified movement-family illustrations, explicitly labeled as illustrations. They are not photographs or exact demonstrations of every variant, and cannot show all safety or technique details. These SVGs are dedicated under CC0 1.0. No demonstration videos are fabricated or implied.

Upute i izvori

Kinra vodiči na engleskom, bosanskom i njemačkom su nezavisno napisane opšte edukativne upute. Klinički protokoli, postoperativni programi i trakcija zahtijevaju individualni plan. Ne tvrdimo da je sadržaj klinički recenziran. Alternative porodice pokreta nisu klinički ekvivalenti.

Pregled dokumentacije od 21. oktobra 2026. dodaje primarne i sekundarne mišićne grupe, stabilizatore, 30 različitih varijanti i osam primjera laganog kretanja koji se mogu uređivati. Mišićni filteri uključuju primarne i sekundarne grupe, a stabilizatori su prikazani odvojeno. Dostavljene teme informisale su nezavisno prepisane upute; izvorni tekst i privatna prepiska nisu objavljeni. Izvornih 778 identiteta i porijeklo ostaju sačuvani. Brojevi pokreta, udaha i prolaza valjkom nisu sekunde niti automatski zabilježeno trajanje.

Fotografije dolaze iz javno dostupne baze free-exercise-db pod licencom Unlicense i čuvaju se lokalno uz izvorne putanje i kontrolne zbirove. Originalne Kinra SVG sheme su ilustracije porodice pokreta pod CC0 1.0, a ne tačne demonstracije svake varijante. Pratite pisane upute za postavku i tehniku.

Anleitungen und Quellen

Die englischen, bosnischen und deutschen Kinra-Anleitungen sind eigenständig verfasste allgemeine Informationen. Klinische Protokolle, postoperative Programme und Traktion benötigen einen individuellen Plan. Eine klinische Prüfung wird nicht behauptet. Optionen derselben Bewegungsfamilie sind keine klinisch gleichwertigen Ersatzübungen.

Die Dokumentationsprüfung vom 21. Oktober 2026 ergänzt primäre und sekundäre Muskelgruppen, Stabilisatoren, 30 unterschiedliche Varianten und acht bearbeitbare Beispiele für sanfte Bewegung. Muskelfilter erfassen primäre und sekundäre Gruppen; Stabilisatoren erscheinen getrennt. Bereitgestellte Themen informierten eigenständig neu formulierte Anleitungen; Quelltexte und private Korrespondenz werden nicht veröffentlicht.
