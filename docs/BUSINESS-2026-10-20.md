# Kinra Business — 20 October 2026

Kinra Business adds a dedicated desktop workspace at `/business` and a phone membership/pass panel in Profile. Parent implemented API, persistence and security; user-requested Astra at medium effort implemented UI using the broader vault design research. The member app and business workspace share identity, not permissions: private business authority comes from ownership or an accepted organization staff invitation.

## Delivered workflows

- Multiple private businesses, branches and capacities; independent member rosters, contact details, numbers and team/group labels. A member need not have a Kinra account. Owner, manager and reception staff permissions are checked server-side on every action.
- Nineteen facility categories: gym, pool, football, combat sports/MMA, sports complex, athletics, basketball, handball, volleyball, tennis, padel, dance, yoga/Pilates, gymnastics, climbing, water sports, winter sports, wellness and configurable other facilities. Pitches, courts, lanes, studios, mats, tracks and rooms support common operational differences.
- Membership plans with duration, price, visit allowance and eligible branches. Issued memberships snapshot plan entitlements; later plan edits do not silently rewrite members' allowances. Date, status, location and actual visit quota determine entry eligibility.
- Reception: camera/keyboard scanner/manual lookup; one-use 90-second opaque QR passes, member status and branch checks, occupancy and checkout. Codes contain no names or health data; only hashes are stored. Duplicate requests return the original receipt; a reused code cannot create another visit.
- Private app invitations with review of the member/business identity, seven-day expiry and single-account linking. Members see their own memberships and may generate passes or book/cancel eligible sessions. Linking does not disclose their health logs.
- Session/resource scheduling, conflict rejection, capacity-limited bookings and cancellation. Business timezone governs membership dates and appointment conversion; nonexistent DST wall times are rejected.
- Immutable invoice records and appended manual payment/refund records, remaining balances and totals. Stable IDs protect retries; concurrent payments cannot overpay. Reception staff receive no finance rows or balances. These are bookkeeping entries, not card charges, fiscal invoices or connected bank balances.
- Full business JSON export; general account export includes business data. Member app erasure unlinks their identity while the facility retains its own records; owner erasure deletes owned businesses and dependent records. UI states explain this scope.
- English, Bosnian and German labels, errors, access decisions, facility/resource choices, dates and controls. Custom names, plan titles and entered content retain their authored language. The populated local business demo uses fictional records.

## Database and implementation

`src/lib/business.ts` defines API/types/validation; `business-api.ts` and `business-demo.ts` provide live/demo implementations through the existing Api interface. `drizzle/migrations/0029_business.sql` adds fifteen scoped tables, RLS and actor-checked atomic RPCs; `0030_business_indexes.sql` covers composite and actor references. Generated isolated migrations target `kinra_v2_` in the existing Kinra Supabase project. Fresh installation and existing-v2 upgrade preserve legacy records. Production additive files: `20261020223000_business.sql` and `20261020224000_business_indexes.sql`; actual hosted schema marker is `0030_business_indexes`.

Mutations serialize on the business row, then validate membership/pass/booking/invoice state in the same transaction. Composite references reject mixed businesses. Raw pass, invitation and attempt tables have no authenticated read grant; protected definer RPCs validate actors and authority. Tokens are absent from audit and export. Audit events record actor/action/target identifiers.

The Supabase advisory pass reports intentional no-policy information for the three private RPC-only tables and authenticated definer warnings for the two guarded APIs. New foreign-key index notices were addressed in 0030. Inherited project findings remain separate: public pg_net extension, existing anonymous/guarded RPCs, leaked-password protection setting and older-table performance notices. This is not independent security certification. References: [RLS guidance](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/postgres/row-level-security), [definer advisory](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/database-linter?lint=0029_authenticated_security_definer_function_executable), [password protection](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/password-security#password-strength-and-leaked-password-protection).

## Evidence and limits

Local tests cover database isolation, plan snapshots, expiration/quota, QR replay/retry, capacities, resource conflicts, immutable ledger, finance RLS, staff revocation, full export and erasure cascades; demo tests exercise concurrent seeding and export/erase isolation. Fresh and upgrade namespace tests include setup/export. UI tests cover EN/BS/DE scanner submission, busy state, business timezone, DST gaps and twelve-month Bosnian date formatting.

Hosted public-client tests used five owned synthetic accounts and real JWTs: **19 assertions passed**, including simultaneous same/different-request QR scans, competing bookings and payments, staff finance RLS, immediate revocation, member unlinking and export. All fixtures completed app erasure and global signout before guarded Auth cleanup. Independent counts found zero fixture Auth, profile, business, roster, audit and Storage rows. Credentials were removed from the workspace audit directory; no real recipients were emailed.

Browser QA: 1440px desktop reception manual entry/checkout; member creation and membership assignment; EN/BS/DE rendering; 390px phone membership panel, QR and actual booking; optical decoding of rendered QR pixels, successful reception submission and German replay denial. The initial Bosnian month-name fallback was fixed with deterministic numeric dates. Component and API coverage is broader than the sampled browser paths; this does not claim every field/device/role combination was browser-tested.

Physical camera capture, USB scanner, glare/autofocus, printed/displayed QR at a real reception and turnstile/door integration still need device acceptance. Entry currently records attendance and does not unlock doors. No payment processing, fiscalization/tax compliance, federation registration, competitive results, guardian consent/minor safeguarding or country-specific operational certification is included. Specialized club workflows and connected devices remain additional scope.

Operational snapshots cap arrays at 2,000 records (member upcoming sessions at 500), display a limit warning and compute summary totals across all records. Full export includes all rows, but an uncapped/paginated historical report and large-facility load validation remain future work. Open visits are prioritized. Ambiguous autumn DST times lack an explicit first/second-occurrence selector. No offline entry authorization is implemented.
