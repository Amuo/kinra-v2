## What changed

- Primary, secondary and stabilizing roles are separated and normalized into useful localized muscle groups. Filters match primary plus secondary targets; stabilizer-only involvement does not match. Source anatomy sections inform 734 original IDs and 15 additions. Additional reviewed corrections cover the 15 unmatched BOSU/Swiss-ball variants, abdominal rollout, chest presses and new variations. Nonspecific workout formats and thermal exposures have no fabricated muscle targets. Lower-leg peroneal/tibialis groups are separated from intrinsic foot muscles.
- Mechanics, laterality and purpose can be inspected and filtered together with existing category, equipment, difficulty and movement filters. Metadata search includes EN/BS/DE labels. These are educational labels, not assessments of an individual's needs.
- A reviewed runtime category override corrects 46 original entries mistakenly captured as isolation exercises despite being multijoint rows, pulldowns or presses. The original evidence file retains every historical category. All 30 new multijoint variations use the compound category directly. Genuine isolated extensions, sissy-squat knee extensions and grip holds keep their isolation classification; unrelated bodyweight, functional and other collections are preserved. Category cards and filters now agree with reviewed mechanics.
- Eighty-three existing guides receive specific, original EN/BS/DE technique notes: machine alignment; fly cable direction and fixed elbow angles; preacher-pad placement; behind-body curl setup; neutral and reverse grips; seated versus lying leg curls; overhead extension control; straight-arm pull mechanics; safe squat depth; hip-thrust lockout; floor-deadlift versus RDL starts; stable row positioning; and bench safety setup. Hip 90/90 is separately corrected: front hip external rotation, rear hip internal rotation. That is **84 existing guides materially enriched**, plus 30 new original guides. Exact affected IDs and names are listed in [exercise-guide-review-impact.json](exercise-guide-review-impact.json).
- Eight short, one-day editable examples cover after-leg, after-upper-body and after-cardio movement, morning mobility, a floor flow, light foam rolling, evening mobility and easy recovery movement. They use the existing `programTemplates` → `localizedTemplate` → editable program flow. These are independently authored examples inspired by routine topics, not copied protocols. Prescriptions count genuine movements, breaths or roller passes; they do not put seconds in a rep field or pretend to log timed sessions. All built-in metadata and notes are EN/BS/DE; catalogue exercise labels retain their stable IDs and follow existing display localization.

## Name review and exclusions

[exercise-heading-decisions.json](exercise-heading-decisions.json) resolves every one of the 52 unmatched source occurrences: 18 aliases of existing entries, 31 occurrences represented by 30 distinct new variations, and three exclusions. “Dumbbell Bench Press” and “FLAT DUMBBELL PRESS” share one new flat-dumbbell identity. Loaded walking lunges and the barbell thruster remain distinct from existing bodyweight and dumbbell versions. Landmine row aliases the existing T-bar row with the same apparatus and path.

Excluded names: generic “Machine Chest Press” is an umbrella for existing narrow/wide machine presses; “Hammer Strength Chest Press” supplies a brand without a distinct machine configuration; “Guillotine Press” lacks an appropriate reviewed teaching progression for a general self-guided neck-directed loaded press. Neither umbrella labels nor a brand create duplicate exercises.

The chest document repeats effectively identical instructions and anatomy across fifty different movements. That technique/anatomy block is not used; the richer isolation text and compound draft inform reviewed distinctions instead. The Hip 90/90 short description reverses the rotations and contradicts its detailed section; the correction above follows the detailed positions. No mandatory depth, universal postoperative schedule, traction instruction, clinical equivalence, DOMS-prevention, cortisol or unsupported therapeutic claim is imported. Existing individual-plan requirements and no-automatic-clinical-alternative boundaries remain.
